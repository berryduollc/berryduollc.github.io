# Privacy Policy for Letter Tangle

**Effective Date:** July 3, 2026

Letter Tangle is designed to be a private, offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal data from users of the app.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, gameplay data, analytics data, advertising identifiers, location data, contact information, account information, device identifiers, or user-generated content.

Letter Tangle does not require an account and does not send your gameplay activity to our servers.

## Offline Use

Letter Tangle is an offline app. Game progress, settings, unlocked content, and hint status are stored locally on your device. We do not receive or access this information.

## In-App Purchases

Letter Tangle may offer in-app purchases, such as unlocking additional levels or purchasing hints. These purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive your payment card information, Apple Account information, billing address, or other payment details.

The app may check purchase status using Apple’s on-device StoreKit system so that purchased content can be unlocked on your device. We do not operate a server for validating purchases and we do not receive purchase records from users.

## Analytics and Tracking

Letter Tangle does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, cookies, or similar technologies.

We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Children’s Privacy

Letter Tangle does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. Because the app does not collect user data, there is no personal information for us to review, delete, or share.

## Data Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties because we do not collect user data.

## Data Retention

We do not retain user data on our servers because we do not collect or receive user data.

Any local app data stored on your device can be removed by deleting the app from your device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Letter Tangle changes in a way that affects privacy, data collection, analytics, purchases, or online features.

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.
